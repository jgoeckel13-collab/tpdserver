TPD Central - kiosk security update 2026-11-24 (server release 2026.11.24.2, kiosk app 20261124c)

AFTER INSTALLING: on the lobby kiosk itself, open Staff setup, sign in, and under "This device" tap
"Register this device as a lobby kiosk". Until you do, visitors there can still fill out and print forms,
but the server will NOT email the officer, send visitor copies, or save their forms. Do this once per kiosk
(and again if the kiosk's browser data is ever cleared). Admins can see and remove kiosks there too.

1. Only a registered kiosk can send email or save forms for visitors. Any other device on the network
   that isn't signed in is refused.
2. Emails from the kiosk to officers are written by the server: "Completed form: <form name> (<date>)".
   Only a real form name is used, so nobody can put their own words or a link into a message sent from
   the TPD address. (Same for the GitHub apps' sending service.)
3. Limits per kiosk: 12 visitor copies per 10 minutes, 3 per email address per hour, 300 emails a day;
   each visitor can send themselves at most 2 copies.
4. Wrong passwords now block that device for 5 minutes (for that username) instead of locking the
   account everywhere, so nobody in the lobby can keep an officer locked out. A flood of 30 wrong
   passwords from many devices still locks the account for 15 minutes.
5. Kiosk privacy: answers are never kept on the kiosk for the next person (no "pick up where you left
   off"), no Download button, and the visitor's email address is never put on the kiosk's clipboard.
6. The app pages tell browsers not to show them inside other websites and to block plugins and
   outside form posts.

TPD Central - update 2026-11-24 (server release 2026.11.24, apps 20261124b)

1. PEOPLE & SIGN-IN (Staff setup, admins only)
   - Every officer on the list has a menu: No sign-in / Officer / Staff / Admin.
     Admin = everything, including this list and email settings. Staff = forms and officers in Staff setup.
     Officer = the officer app only.
   - Giving someone a sign-in creates a one-time setup link (works once, expires in 7 days). If server email
     is on, it is emailed to the officer's address; you can also copy it and text or hand it to them.
     They open it, see their username (from their email, e.g. jgoeckel) and choose their own password.
     Admins never see or type anyone's password.
   - "Send password reset link" gives someone who forgot their password a new link. An admin can no longer
     type a new password for another person.
   - "Someone not on the officer list" adds a sign-in for anyone else (e.g. a records clerk).
   - Accounts made before this update keep working; they show under "Other sign-ins".
   - Setup links only open on the department network. Start the server with "Start TPD Central (network)"
     so links use the server's network address instead of localhost.
   - You cannot change or remove your own admin role (so there is always at least one admin).
2. VERSION NUMBERS: the bottom of every screen shows "App version 20261124b · Server 2026.11.24".
   After an update each device shows "Updated to version ... (was ...)" with what's new, once.
   When you update the server, change SERVER_RELEASE at the top of tpd_central.py.
3. FIXED: one device that connected but stalled (Wi-Fi drop, port scan) could freeze the whole server in
   HTTPS mode until it let go. The secure handshake now happens per connection with a 15-second limit.
4. Officer app: Recent forms are deleted right at 24 hours (also in the background on installed Android
   apps), plus a "Delete all now" button. The kiosk and officer apps match the GitHub versions (20261124b).

TPD Central - security update (2026-11-10)

AFTER INSTALLING THIS UPDATE
1. Replace the certificate authority once (the old one could vouch for any website):
     Start TPD Central.bat --https --lan --new-ca
   Then on every computer/tablet/phone, remove the old "TPD Local CA" certificate and
   install the new one from https://<server>/setup.
   If the server PC's name or address ever changes, the window will say so; run --new-ca again.
2. Email sending > new switch "Let visitors email themselves a copy (kiosk)". It starts ON.
3. The first admin account can now only be created on the server computer itself
   (use the localhost address there). This also applies after --reset-users.
4. Changing a password now asks for your current password.
5. Officer app: Sign out now also deletes Recent forms and any unfinished form on that device.
   If the server can't be reached and the device's sign-in is older than 12 hours, the app locks.
6. Optional: to stop loading the car-seat label reader from the internet, put tesseract.min.js,
   worker.min.js, the tesseract-core *.wasm.js files and eng.traineddata.gz (tesseract.js 5.1.1)
   in central\officer\vendor\ocr\ . The app uses that copy when it is there.

Never commit central\data or cert\ to GitHub (the .gitignore file now blocks them).

SAVED FORMS (2026-11-15)
- Staff setup (admin) > "Saved forms": turn on saving and pick how long to keep forms:
  1 week, 2 weeks, 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1, 2 or 3 years, or until an admin deletes them.
  Choose whether kiosk forms, officer-app forms or both are saved, and whether officers see every saved
  form or only the ones they completed (staff and admins always see all). It starts OFF.
- Officer app home > "Saved forms": search, open, re-print, email to officers. Admins can delete.
- Forms are deleted automatically when the period runs out (checked every 10 minutes and at start-up).
  Shortening the period deletes older forms right away (you are asked first).
- Copies live in central\data\saved and are backed up with the data folder. Every save, open, email and
  delete is written to central\data\audit.log. Saving stops if that folder reaches 20 GB.
- These are working copies. Keep the official copy in the records system, and keep the server's drive
  encrypted (BitLocker), since saved forms contain personal details.
